Passive Voice

⏱️ 7 min de leitura +100 XP

Introducao

A voz passiva coloca o foco em quem RECEBE a acao.

Formacao

Sujeito + be + past participle (+ by agente)

Exemplos

AtivaPassiva
They build housesHouses are built (by them)
She wrote a bookA book was written (by her)
They will fix itIt will be fixed

Resumo

  • Be + past participle. Foco no objeto.
  • ENEM: passiva em textos jornalisticos e cientificos

Curiosidade

Textos cientificos usam MUITO passiva: 'The experiment was conducted...' - o foco e no experimento, nao em quem fez!

Essencial para a Prova

Voz ativa: sujeito faz acao (The cat ate the fish). Voz passiva: sujeito recebe acao (The fish was eaten by the cat). Formacao: be + past participle. By + agente (opcional). Usos: quando o OBJETO e mais importante, agente desconhecido, textos cientificos, jornalisticos. Tempos: is/are + pp (present), was/were + pp (past), will be + pp (future), has been + pp (present perfect).

Dúvidas Frequentes

Quando o agente e desconhecido (My car was stolen), quando o objeto importa mais (The Mona Lisa was painted by Da Vinci), textos formais.

Nao! So use 'by + agente' se o agente for RELEVANTE. 'The window was broken' (nao importa por quem). 'America was discovered by Columbus' (importa).

Has/have been + past participle. 'The email has been sent.' 'The books have been delivered.'

Sim, tende a ser mais formal. Textos academicos, jornalisticos, cientificos preferem passiva. Conversas informais preferem ativa.
