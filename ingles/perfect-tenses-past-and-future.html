Perfect Tenses: Past and Future

⏱️ 7 min de leitura +150 XP

Introducao

Os perfect tenses avancados marcam acoes anteriores a marcos temporais.

Past Perfect

  • Had + past participle
  • 'When I arrived, she had left.'
  • Acao ANTES de outra no passado

Future Perfect

  • Will have + past participle
  • 'By 2030, I will have graduated.'
  • Acao completa ANTES de ponto futuro

Resumo

  • Past Perfect: antes no passado. Future Perfect: completo no futuro.
  • ENEM: Past Perfect em narrativas

Curiosidade

O Past Perfect e chamado de 'passado do passado' - um flashback temporal!

Essencial para a Prova

Past Perfect: had + pp. Acao ANTES de outra no passado. 'When I arrived, she had already left.' (Ela saiu antes, eu cheguei depois). Future Perfect: will have + pp. Acao completa ANTES de um momento futuro. 'By 2030, I will have graduated.' Marcadores: before, after, when, by, already, never.

Dúvidas Frequentes

Simple Past: acao no passado. Past Perfect: acao ANTES de outra no passado. 'She left' vs 'She had left (before I arrived)'.

Menos comum no dia a dia, mas aparece em textos formais. 'By next year, the project will have been completed.'

Marcador do Future Perfect. 'By December, by 2030, by the time you arrive.' Indica 'ate quando' a acao estara completa.

Usado para flashbacks. 'He was sad because his dog had died.' (Cachorro morreu ANTES de ele ficar triste).
