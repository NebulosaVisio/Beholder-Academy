Reported Speech (Indirect Speech)

⏱️ 7 min de leitura +100 XP

Introducao

O reported speech transforma fala direta em indireta.

Mudancas

DirectReported
am/iswas
arewere
willwould
cancould
todaythat day
herethere

Exemplo

  • Direct: 'I will come tomorrow.'
  • Reported: He said he would come the next day.

Resumo

  • Verbos recuam 1 tempo. Pronomes mudam.
  • ENEM: interpretar reported speech em textos

Curiosidade

Em ingles, o 'that' do reported speech e OPCIONAL! 'She said (that) she was tired.'

Essencial para a Prova

Direct: 'I am happy,' she said. Reported: She said (that) she was happy. Mudancas: am/is→was, are→were, will→would, can→could, present→past. Pronomes mudam conforme o contexto. Tempo: today→that day, yesterday→the day before, tomorrow→the next day. Here→there. Reporting verbs: said, told, asked, explained.

Dúvidas Frequentes

Quando o reporting verb esta no passado (said, told), sim. Se esta no presente (says, tells), nao precisa mudar.

Said: sem complemento ('She said...'). Told: com complemento ('She told ME...'). Never: 'She said me' (ERRADO!).

Mudam para ordem afirmativa. 'Where do you live?' → He asked where I lived. Sem ponto de interrogacao!

'Study hard!' → She told me TO STUDY hard. 'Don't go!' → He told me NOT TO GO. Usar to + infinitive.
