Review: All Basic Tenses

⏱️ 7 min de leitura +100 XP

Introducao

Os tempos verbais basicos sao a fundacao do ingles.

Tempos

TempoUsoExemplo
Simple PresentRotinas, fatosShe studies every day
Simple PastAcoes concluidasShe studied yesterday
Simple FuturePrevisoes, decisoesShe will study tomorrow
Present ContinuousAgoraShe is studying now
Past ContinuousEm andamento no passadoShe was studying

Resumo

  • Present: rotina. Past: concluido. Future: will/going to.
  • ENEM: tempos verbais em contexto de leitura

Curiosidade

O ingles tem 12 tempos verbais, mas no dia a dia usamos cerca de 5-6 com frequencia!

Essencial para a Prova

Simple Present: rotinas (I study). Simple Past: acoes concluidas (I studied). Simple Future: will + verbo (I will study), going to (planos). Present Continuous: agora (I am studying). Past Continuous: acao em andamento no passado (I was studying). Auxiliares: do/does, did, will.

Dúvidas Frequentes

Rotinas (I wake up at 7), fatos (Water boils at 100C), horarios fixos (The train leaves at 9).

Will: decisoes espontaneas, previsoes. Going to: planos ja decididos. 'I will help you' (agora decidi) vs 'I'm going to travel' (ja planejei).

Past Continuous: acao EM ANDAMENTO quando outra aconteceu. 'I was sleeping WHEN the phone rang.' Sleeping=cont, rang=simple.

Do/does (present), did (past), will (future). Usados em perguntas e negativas. 'Do you study?' 'She doesn't work.' 'Did they go?'
